About Me

Most of what I build comes back to one thing: figuring out why something broke. I started fixing computers for people in 2021, and I still think about software the same way I think about a laptop that won't turn on. What could cause this, how do I check, and what's the fix?

That's where Troubleshooter came from. It's basically the questions I ask when someone hands me a broken device, turned into an app. It's also why, on my AI-built projects, I care less about the happy path and more about what happens when things go wrong: a crash in the middle of a task, a server that stops answering, a ledger that has to come back correct after a restart.

I learned to code by hand in high school, making games with a friend. Now I'm a first-year CS student at UIUC, and I'm trying to get better at the parts AI can't do for me yet: deciding what a program should do, and checking that it really does it.

Troubleshooter

My CS 124 Honors project. You pick a device and what's wrong with it, answer a few questions, and it guesses the most likely causes and shows you how to fix it. Device names come from iFixit, and the repair steps link back to where they came from.

People can vote on whether a fix worked for them, and fixes with better results move up the list. It also has accounts, a color-blind mode, and a version that works without JavaScript.

React · TypeScript · Vite · Firebase · MapLibre

Multi-Agent Task Coordination System In progress

A tool for running several AI coding agents at once without them stepping on each other. Tasks can depend on other tasks, and if something crashes or times out, it retries or picks back up after a restart instead of starting over.

I spent most of my time on the failure cases: what should happen when a provider goes down halfway through a task, and how to make sure a task never runs twice.

React · TypeScript · Node.js · SQLite

CS2 Market Analytics & Paper Trading In progress

A tool for tracking Counter-Strike 2 skin prices. It pulls market data, keeps track of an inventory, and lets me test trading ideas with fake money on past data.
