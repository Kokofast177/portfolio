About Me

I'm a first-year Computer Science student at the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign, currently taking CS 124 Honors. I got into computing by fixing them: I've been repairing computers since 2021 and spent two years keeping 400+ Chromebooks running for my school district.

That hands-on side still drives what I build. I run a homelab of three repurposed servers, and my software projects lean toward systems that have to stay reliable: durable task queues, ledgers, recovery after crashes. I use AI coding tools heavily, and my focus is on writing clear specs and test criteria and then verifying the result through code review and testing.

I'm looking for internships where I can work on real systems, learn from experienced engineers, and keep building.
